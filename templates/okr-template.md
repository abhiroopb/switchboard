## Before you agree them
- **Results, not tasks.** Each key result describes a change you can measure. "Launch the new setup flow" is a task. "Cut median setup time from 12 to 5 minutes" is a result.
- **Measurable today.** The baseline exists or the team knows how it will get one in the first two weeks.
- **A stretch.** The team is unsure it can hit every target. Hitting all of them easily means the targets were safe.
- **No bad incentives.** Pair a result that can be gamed with a guardrail metric that would catch it.
- **Few of them.** Two or three objectives, each with two to four key results.

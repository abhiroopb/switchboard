## Warm-up (about 5 minutes)
- **Introductions.** Explain the purpose in plain words. Say there are no right answers and that we are here to learn from them.
- **Context.** Tell me about your role and what a typical week looks like.
- **Starting point.** Walk me through how {the task} fits into your work.

## Core questions (most of the session)
Ask about specific past events. Follow the participant's story, and use the prompts below as a loose guide.

### Topic 1: {Name}
- **Last time.** Tell me about the last time you {did the task}. What happened, step by step?
- **Hard parts.** What was the hardest or most annoying part of that?
- **Workarounds.** What did you do to get around it?

### Topic 2: {Name}
- **Current approach.** Walk me through how you handle {situation} today.
- **Tools and people.** Who else was involved, and which tools did you use?
- **Cost.** What did that cost you in time, money, or effort?

### Topic 3: {Name}
- **Past attempts.** Have you tried other ways to solve this? What happened?
- **Decision.** What made you choose your current approach?
- **Change.** If that part of your work changed tomorrow, what would be different for you?

**Follow-up prompts:**
- **Go deeper.** Tell me more about that.
- **Get specific.** Can you show me, or walk me through an example?
- **Find the why.** Why was that important to you?
- **Stay quiet.** Pause and let the participant fill the silence.

**Avoid:**
- **Leading questions.** Keep the team's preferred answer out of the question.
- **Hypotheticals.** Asking whether someone would use a feature predicts poorly. Ask what they did last time.
- **Pitching.** Hold off on showing or selling ideas until the end, if at all.

## Wrap-up (about 5 minutes)
- **Missed topics.** What else should we know that we did not ask about?
- **Biggest wish.** If you could change one thing about how you {do the task}, what would it be?
- **Next steps.** Thank them, explain any incentive, and ask whether we may follow up.
